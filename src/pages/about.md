# About Adel Nizamutdinov

I am a software engineer and product-minded consultant based in Kazan,
Tatarstan. I build and maintain products where good engineering judgment
matters more than ceremony: web applications, backend services, APIs,
developer tools, internal software, and integrations that have to survive real
use. My public work includes Listenbox, a podcast-hosting product; Arrowbox, a
flowchart editor; and ResponsibleAPI, which generates SDKs, documentation,
MCP servers, and CLIs from OpenAPI contracts.

This site is my working record. The [writing section](/#writing-heading) explains decisions,
tradeoffs, and experiments. The [CV](/cv/) gives the longer career history, and
the [consulting page](/consulting/) describes the kinds of projects I can own.
I also publish a [travel timeline](/travel/) and a short [now page](/now/) when
current context is useful.

For a concrete project, start with the [contact page](/contact/). Business is
conducted through Pneuma LLC. I prefer a clear problem statement, the current
constraints, and evidence of what has already been tried; that gives both sides
enough context to decide whether a focused engagement makes sense.
