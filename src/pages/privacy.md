# Privacy

This is a small, mostly static personal website. It does not require an
account, offer a public form, or intentionally collect a profile about
visitors. The site does not use advertising pixels or third-party analytics in
the page content. Cloudflare Pages may process ordinary request data such as
an IP address, user agent, timestamp, and requested resource as part of
delivering and protecting the site; those records are controlled by the
hosting provider and are not used here to build a personal advertising profile.

If you email [mail@adelnz.com](mailto:mail@adelnz.com), your message and the
contact details you include are used to reply and, when relevant, discuss a
potential project. I keep that correspondence only as long as it is useful for
the conversation or an ongoing business relationship, subject to applicable
law. Do not send confidential credentials, private keys, or sensitive personal
data in an initial message.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Listenbox, Arrowbox, ResponsibleAPI, and other sites
lead to services with their own privacy practices. This page explains this
site's practice, not theirs. Questions or requests about personal data can be
sent to the email address above. Business is conducted through Pneuma LLC.
